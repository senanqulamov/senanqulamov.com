# Building Better Live Experiences

BidBary is an all-in-one event platform for discovering, booking, hosting, and managing live experiences. It connects attendees with concerts, auctions, parties, tours, workshops, exhibitions, and more—without forcing organizers and guests to move between disconnected tools.

## One journey for every attendee

Discovering an event, choosing a ticket, selecting a seat, completing payment, and accessing a digital ticket should feel like one connected experience. BidBary brings those steps together with authentication, favorites, booking, seat selection, and multilingual navigation.

## Tools for the people running the event

Organizers need more than a public listing. BidBary gives them event creation and editing tools, ticket tiers, media management, seatmap configuration, attendee management, analytics, and check-in workflows. Administrators can moderate events while organizers stay in control of their operations.

## Built for the moment at the door

The experience does not end after payment. Digital tickets and QR-based validation support a reliable check-in flow for attendees and event teams. Auctions remain a supported event format, alongside fixed-price ticketing and capacity-based booking.

## The product challenge

The hardest part is keeping discovery, availability, booking, payments, organizer tooling, and admission consistent across attendee, organizer, and administrator roles. The interface is built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Konva, Next-intl, and JWT-backed authentication to make that complexity feel straightforward.

BidBary is designed to make live experiences easier to find, book, host, and run.
